## How this feeds the public page

Once a field above is genuinely answered and Aisha approves exact public wording:

1. Record the claim, its evidence and the approved wording in
   `docs/about-professional-experience-verification.md`'s table (status: "Owner confirmed" or
   "Evidence confirmed", never inferred from this intake alone).
2. Add the fact to `content/about.ts`'s `professionalStory` narrative or context blocks, or — for
   a dated milestone — to `professionalStory.milestones` as a `VerifiedMilestone` record, only once
   at least two such milestones qualify (see that file's own "Timeline decision" comment).
3. Never skip step 1 — a code change without a corresponding verification-record entry is not
   traceable and should not be merged.

Nothing in this file is itself a public claim. A blank field means the claim stays unpublished.
